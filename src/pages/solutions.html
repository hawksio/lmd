Business finance

Finance shaped around your business

Three common types of business finance, what each one is for, and what to ask before you apply.

[Product range, eligibility criteria, amounts, terms, rates and fees to be confirmed by LMD Finance Ltd before launch.] The descriptions below explain how each type of finance generally works. They are not an offer, and availability depends on your circumstances.

Business loans

Business loans

A fixed sum repaid in regular instalments over an agreed term, often used for expansion, refurbishment, stock or hiring.

  • Ask for the total amount repayable, not just the monthly payment
  • Check whether security or a personal guarantee is required
  • Check any early repayment charges
Ask about a business loan
Asset finance

Equipment, vehicles & technology

Spreads the cost of an asset over its working life, so cash stays available for day-to-day running.

  • Understand who owns the asset during and at the end of the agreement
  • Check any final or balloon payment
  • Have the supplier's quote ready
Ask about asset finance
Cash-flow finance

Invoice finance & working capital

Helps bridge the gap between doing the work and getting paid.

  • Check how fees are charged (per invoice, monthly, or both)
  • Ask whether your customers will be told about the arrangement
  • Check the notice period to end the facility
Ask about cash-flow finance
