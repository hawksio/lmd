How it works

Clear steps, and you always know where you stand

Every application has a status you can check in the client portal at any time.

Tell us about your business

Send an enquiry or create an account and start an application. You can save a draft and come back to it.

We review your application

We check what you've told us and may ask for documents, such as bank statements or accounts, through the portal.

  • Secure document upload in your client portal
  • Messages with the team in one place
  • An email whenever the status changes

A decision, in writing

If the application is approved, you'll receive the terms and any conditions in writing. Approval is not a payment: nothing is paid until an agreement is signed.

Agreement and funding

You review and sign the agreement. Funding is then scheduled, and the portal shows "Funds disbursed" only once the payment has actually been made and checked.

Application statuses

What each status means

Application submitted
We have your application. This is not a decision.
Under review
The team is assessing your application.
Additional information requested
We need more information or documents. Check your messages in the portal.
Approved, subject to conditions
A decision to offer finance on stated terms, subject to conditions. No agreement exists yet and no money has been paid.
Agreement issued
An agreement is ready for you to review. Read it carefully and ask questions before signing.
Agreement signed
We have received your signed agreement.
Funding scheduled
Payment is being arranged. It has not been made yet.
Funds disbursed
The payment has been made and checked against our bank records.
Declined or withdrawn
The application has closed. You can ask us why it was declined.
