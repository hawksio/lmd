How we work

Conversation first, paperwork second

We start by understanding the business: what the finance is for, how the business is performing and what repayments would be comfortable. Only then do we look at the options.

We explain each option in plain English, including the total cost, the repayments and any security or conditions, so you can make a decision with the full picture.
