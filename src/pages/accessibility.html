Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and the client portal. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Text and background colours are chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are explained in words next to the field.
  • Images have text alternatives.
  • Animations are switched off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.

Known limitations

  • Some PDF letters may not be fully accessible to screen readers. Ask us for another format.
  • This site has not yet had an independent accessibility audit. [Date of audit to be added.]
