Cookie policy

Draft for legal review. Accurate for the site as built. Update it if analytics, advertising or other third-party tools are added.

We use only what is strictly necessary for the website and client portal to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't ask for cookie consent.

Fonts

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. [Decision needed: keep this, or host the fonts on our own server to avoid it.]

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the session cookie, you won't be able to sign in or send the contact form.
