# BRIEF: LMD Finance Ltd home page

Interviewed 2026-10-19 (multiple-choice interview in chat). Answers where the
client chose an option are recorded as chosen; everything else is an authored
decision, marked as such. Carries forward the earlier photography brief:
realistic human photography, navy / white / champagne gold, no invented staff.

> **Update 2026-10-19:** the client replaced navy / white / champagne gold ("this design color is too
> popular"), trying forest / brass and aubergine / sage, and settled on midnight teal / white / coral. Where
> this brief says navy or gold, read teal or coral. Structure, feeling curve and peak are unchanged.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Client chose: "Calm, premium, trustworthy. Restrained motion, lots
   of space, navy and gold. Feels like an established private bank: confident,
   never flashy." References (authored): a private-bank annual report, a
   well-run high-street solicitor's waiting room, a Monocle business feature.
2. **Journey (authored from the existing site and the brief).** Welcome from a
   real team, being understood, seeing the options, meeting the people and the
   process, watching a plan take shape, then making that plan their own.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm almost everywhere. One build of intensity at the plan.
   Quiet again for the close.
4. **Feeling / the one moment.** Client chose: "Watching their plan take shape.
   As they scroll, a funding plan assembles live: amount, term and monthly cost
   build up step by step, with an adviser photo alongside."
5. **Unique thing.** Authored: the plan you watched being written is handed to
   you to edit, and it travels with you into the enquiry form.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal, chosen by the client, not defaulted.
7. **World.** Client chose: "Distinct chapters", hard cuts, not one world.
8. **Assets.** 18 licensed Unsplash photographs already in `public/images`, the
   SVG monogram. No footage, no generation key, so no scrub video. Photographic
   world, real people, no generated imagery.

Scope: client chose "Home page only". Inner pages keep the existing layout.

## Grammar

**Named new grammar: Consultation.** The page is organised as the stages of a
first meeting with an adviser.

- Sections are meeting stages, each on its own ground with a hard cut (no drift).
- Nav: the site's own header, because this is one page of a multi-page site and
  the inner pages need to be reachable. It stays plain on purpose.
- Hero: a layered photographic welcome, not a title page and not a scrub.
- Close: an operable plan (real inputs, real arithmetic), with the CTA inside the
  plan rather than on a separate button island.
- Bans: `scrub`, `magnet`, `spotlight`, `drift`, invented statistics, counters
  that claim anything about the company, centred hero copy.

Why the eight defined grammars lost:
- Filmic one-shot: client asked for chapters, not one continuous film.
- Chaptered editorial: its title-page hero bans media above the fold, which
  breaks the client's standing requirement for a large staff photo in the hero.
- Live surface: bans full-bleed photography and marketing chrome, and the
  photography brief is core to this site.
- Continuous world: client chose distinct chapters.
- Typographic poster: bans the photography the brand depends on.
- Gallery / catalog: three finance products are not a collection to walk; the
  visitor's question is "can you help me", not "what are the options".
- Split stage: there is no two-sided argument here.
- Rhythmic cutlist: the opposite of calm.

## Journey

1. Welcome: a real team, already in the room.
2. Understood: the page names their situation plainly.
3. Choice: three kinds of finance, laid out sideways.
4. People: the advisers and how a meeting runs.
5. The plan: it writes itself out as they scroll (peak).
6. Ownership: the plan becomes theirs to adjust, and they book.

## Feeling curve

1. **Welcome / calm**: a layered office photo where the team sits deeper than
   the headline and a consultation photo floats forward over the edge.
2. **Understood / recognition**: navy ground, type only, three short lines about
   their situation, nothing moving but the words.
3. **Breadth / choice**: three finance types travelling sideways under the
   scroll, each with a photograph and one fact.
4. **Trust / warmth**: real people at work, photographs wiping open, a plain
   description of how the meeting goes. Quiet on purpose: the silence before the
   peak.
5. **Clarity / relief (peak)**: a paper plan on the right fills in line by line,
   gold rules drawing under each entry, figures counting up, while the photos on
   the left move from talking to comparing to signing. Ends on "Ready to apply".
6. **Resolve / ownership**: the same plan, now with sliders. Their numbers, their
   repayment, one button that carries it to the enquiry form.

## The peak

"I scrolled and a funding plan wrote itself out next to photos of the meeting,
and then it let me change the numbers." Lives in act 5. Gets the largest span on
the page (4.2 viewport-heights) and the quiet flow section in front of it.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you watch your funding plan get written out for you, and
then it hands you the pen.

## Authored silence

None. Every act carries content from its first frame.

## Honesty constraints

- Plan figures are an example, labelled "Example" on its face. The monthly
  repayment is real arithmetic (standard amortisation) at an illustrative 9.9%
  APR that is shown on screen and must be confirmed with compliance before
  launch.
- No company statistics anywhere on the page.
- Stock models are never named.
