# Fingerprints

Registry for scroll-craft builds in this project. A new build must differ from EVERY row
below on at least 4 of the 6 dimensions (grammar, nav treatment, hero device, act-sequence
shape, close pattern, signature move). Rows are append-only.

## The registry

| Build | Grammar | Nav treatment | Hero device | Act-sequence shape | Close pattern | Signature move | World | Port |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lmd-home (2026-10-19) | Consultation (named new): sections are the stages of a first adviser meeting, hard cuts between navy / paper / white grounds | The site's own multi-page header (plain, sticky); no page-specific chrome | Pinned layered photo: team photo bed recedes and scales down, a consultation photo card floats forward over it, copy on a masked navy column | pin(parallax) > flow(kinetic) > pan(5-item rail) > flow(reveal + parallax) > pin(bespoke, peak) > flow(form); 6 acts, about 12.6vh desktop | An operable plan form (sliders, live arithmetic) with the CTA inside the plan; the plan travels to the enquiry form | A funding plan that writes itself out row by row (gold rules drawing, figures counting) beside photos of the meeting, then is handed over to edit | Photographic, licensed stock of a UK finance office, navy / white / champagne gold | localhost:4321 |

## What is taken

- Consultation grammar (meeting stages as chapters, hard-cut grounds).
- Close as an operable document with the CTA inside it, carried to the next page.
- Signature: a document that fills itself in under the scroll, then becomes editable.
- Hero: masked photo bed + forward-floating inset card, copy on the masked side.
