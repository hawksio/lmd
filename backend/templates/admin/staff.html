What each role can do

Organisation administrator
Everything in this organisation: staff, products, financial settings, organisation settings, content, marketing, decisions and payments (never confirming their own entries).
Underwriter
Review applications and documents, propose and issue decisions (a different person issues large approvals), prepare letters.
Document reviewer
Open, verify and reject documents and request evidence. Cannot make lending decisions.
Customer support
Message customers, handle enquiries, edit (not publish) website content. Cannot open customer documents.
Finance officer
Schedule, record and confirm payments (not their own), repayment schedules and repayments, fees, reports and exports.
Read-only auditor
View applications, documents, reports and the audit log. Cannot change anything.
