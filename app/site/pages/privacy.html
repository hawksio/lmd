What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, business name, email, optional phone number, the type of finance you're interested in and your message.
  • Client portal account: your name, email, optional phone number, a securely hashed password, and optional two-step verification settings.
  • Applications: business details (name, type, Companies House number, industry, time trading, turnover, address), your role in the business, and the finance requested (type, amount, term, purpose).
  • Documents you upload: for example bank statements, accounts and proof of identity.
  • Messages between you and our team in the portal.
  • Security and audit records: sign-in times, the IP address and browser type of active sessions, and a record of significant actions (for example who viewed or downloaded a document).
  • Marketing preference: only if you choose to opt in, with the date and the wording you agreed to.

How we use it, and our lawful bases

[Lawful bases to be confirmed by the data-protection adviser.] We use the information to respond to enquiries, assess and administer applications, carry out checks you consent to (which may include a credit search), keep the portal secure, keep records required by law, and, only if you opted in, send marketing emails.

Who we share it with

[Recipients to be confirmed, e.g. lenders, credit reference and fraud prevention agencies, IT and email service providers, professional advisers.] We do not sell personal data.

If an optional AI help assistant is switched on in the client portal, the text of questions you type into it (with email addresses and numbers removed) is sent to our AI service provider to generate an answer. Application details and documents are never sent to it.

How long we keep it

[Retention periods to be confirmed.] Expired sign-in links and old session records are deleted automatically.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Documents are stored outside the public website, under random file names, and can only be opened by you and by authorised staff after signing in. Staff access is limited by role and significant actions are recorded in an audit log.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format, subject to legal limits. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe link or in Account and security. To make a request, contact {{mailto:email}}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
